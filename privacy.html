食霧

記錄自己吃過的每一家店和每道菜。

隱私權政策

「食霧」不收集你的任何資料。

這個 App 沒有帳號、沒有註冊、沒有伺服器、沒有廣告、沒有分析工具、沒有追蹤，也沒有任何第三方追蹤或分析 SDK。你記下的每一家店、每一道菜、每一張照片，都存在你自己的手機裡，開發者無從得知。

資料存在哪裡

所有記錄——店家、每一餐、菜與評價、心得、同行者、想去的店——都存在你這支手機的 App 本機資料庫。照片存在 App 自己的檔案空間（Application Support），資料庫裡只記檔名。偏好設定（常一起吃的人、排序方式、已達成的里程碑）存在本機的 UserDefaults。

這些資料不會離開你的裝置，除非你自己按下匯出。刪除 App 就會一併刪掉這些資料，開發者手上不會留下任何副本。

定位

只有在你記一餐、需要列出附近的店家時，App 才會向你要一次目前位置，用的是使用 App 期間（When In Use）的權限。

  • 一次只取一次位置，不做連續定位、不做移動追蹤，也沒有任何背景執行模式。背景取得位置只有在你自己打開「停留提醒」之後才會發生，說明在下一節。
  • 精度刻意設在約 100 公尺這一級，夠找出你在哪一家店就好。
  • 位置只用來在當下找出附近的店家。你選定店家之後，存進記錄的是那家店的座標，不是你的座標。
  • 例外只有一個：當你自己打店名建立一家系統上找不到的店時，這家店的座標會直接用你當下的位置（約 100 公尺精度）標記。App 在那個畫面上有寫明「自訂店家 · 位置以目前所在標記」。這筆座標一樣只存在你的裝置上。
  • 在系統的「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 定位服務」可以關掉。關掉之後 App 仍然可以用，只是要自己搜尋店名。

停留提醒（選用）

這個功能預設是關的。沒有打開之前，App 不會建立任何背景的位置監聽。

打開之後：

  • 用的是系統的三種機制，都由系統判斷、由系統把 App 叫醒：造訪偵測（Visit Monitoring，停留與離開由系統判斷）、顯著位置變更（走遠了才叫醒 App）、店家範圍偵測（進出想去清單與常去店家周圍 80 公尺的範圍時叫醒 App，同時最多 20 家）。App 本身不進行連續定位、也不記錄你的移動路線。
  • 到達一個地方時 App 會取一次位置（高精度，最多等 20 秒），用來判斷你在哪一家店。造訪偵測給的座標常常有一百公尺以上的誤差。
  • 需要永遠允許（Always）的定位權限與通知權限。任一項沒有取得，開關就會回到關閉。
  • 判斷你離開的依據有三種：造訪偵測的離開事件、離停留地點至少 150 公尺的位置變更（定位誤差大時跟著放寬）、走出店家範圍。收到之後 App 會在你的裝置上判斷要不要問你一句：停留時間是否落在 15 分鐘到 4 小時之間、當時附近有沒有餐飲店家、那段時間你是不是已經自己記過一餐、當天問過幾次，以及是不是深夜。
  • 查詢附近店家會送出那次停留的座標給 Apple 地圖服務，用途與記錄流程的附近清單相同（見「地圖」一節）。查詢結果不會離開你的裝置。
  • 通知會問你要不要記下這一餐。按了通知只是把記錄表單填好，並不會建立任何記錄。
  • App 在裝置上保留的有：這個開關的狀態、今天問過幾次、最近 40 次停留的辨識碼（座標取到小數第四位、時間取到分鐘），用來避免同一次停留問你兩遍；還沒問完的停留（到達時間、座標與查到的候選店家）最多留 12 筆、24 小時；最後一次位置更新的經緯度一組，用來決定該在哪幾家店周圍畫範圍；以及最近 300 筆事件紀錄（到達、離開、位置更新、進出範圍、通知），每一筆帶有當時的座標與定位誤差，用來查這個功能有沒有算錯。這些都存在你的裝置上，不會離開，也不會寫進你的記錄。
  • 在 App 的「設定 → 提醒」可以關掉；關掉之後所有位置監聽都會停止。

照片

照片來自你自己從相簿挑選的照片，或當下用相機拍的相片。

  • 相簿是透過系統的照片選取器開啟的，App 只拿得到你選的那幾張，不會瀏覽你的相簿，也從來沒有要求過相簿的完整讀取權限。
  • 選到的照片會縮圖後複製一份到 App 的本機檔案空間，之後在 App 內顯示的是這一份。
  • 從相簿補記舊帳時，App 會讀取照片本身帶的拍攝日期與 GPS 座標：日期用來自動帶入那一餐的日期，座標用來找出照片附近有哪些店。這個讀取全部在你的裝置上完成，照片與其中的資訊不會被上傳；照片的 GPS 座標本身不會存進資料庫。
  • App 不會把照片寫回你的相簿。

地圖

地圖畫面、搜尋店家、反查行政區與導航入口，使用的是 Apple 內建的地圖服務（MapKit 與 CoreLocation）。這是 Apple 提供的系統服務，以下三種情況會由系統向 Apple 發出請求：

  1. 顯示地圖（取得地圖圖磚）。
  2. 搜尋店家時，你打的字與當時的位置範圍會送給 Apple，用來回傳建議與附近的餐廳清單。
  3. 你自己建立的店家若缺縣市／行政區資料，該店座標會送給 Apple 換回行政區名稱。

這些請求由 Apple 處理，受 Apple 的隱私權政策規範。開發者不會收到、也看不到這些請求的內容。

除此之外，這個 App 不會為了任何理由連上任何伺服器——開發者沒有伺服器。

備份與匯出

App 可以把全部記錄匯出成一個 JSON 檔，或把記錄與照片打包成一個完整備份檔，也可以把備份檔匯入還原。

  • 匯出只在你主動按下的時候發生。
  • 產生的檔案會交給系統的分享面板，存到哪裡、傳給誰，完全由你決定。
  • 備份檔沒有密碼保護，內容就是你的記錄與照片，請自行決定要放在哪裡。
  • 開發者不會收到這個檔案，也沒有任何自動上傳或雲端同步（App 沒有使用 iCloud 同步）。

Privacy Policy (English)

Shiwu does not collect any of your data.

There is no account, no sign-up, no server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. Every place, dish, note and photo you record stays on your own device. The developer has no way to see any of it.

Where your data lives

All of your records — places, meals, dishes and ratings, notes, companions, wishlist entries — are stored in the app's local database on your device. Photos are stored in the app's own file storage (Application Support); the database only keeps file names. Preferences (frequent dining companions, sort order, milestones reached) are stored locally in UserDefaults.

None of this leaves your device unless you choose to export it. Deleting the app deletes this data. The developer keeps no copy.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you are logging a meal and it needs to list nearby restaurants. It uses When In Use authorization only.

  • It takes a single location fix at that moment. No continuous location updates, no movement tracking, and no background execution modes at all. Background location access happens only if you turn on Dwell Reminders yourself — see the next section.
  • Accuracy is deliberately set to roughly 100 metres — enough to work out which restaurant you are in.
  • Your location is used at that moment to find nearby places. Once you pick a place, what gets saved with the record is that place's coordinates, not yours.
  • There is one exception: if you type in a place the map service doesn't know, that place is marked at your current location (at the same ~100 m accuracy). The app says so on screen. Those coordinates also stay on your device.
  • Location can be turned off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services. The app still works; you just search for places by name instead.

Dwell Reminders (optional)

This feature is off by default. Until you turn it on, the app creates no background location monitoring of any kind.

Once it is on:

  • It uses three system services, all of which decide on their own when to wake the app: Visit Monitoring (the system decides when you have arrived and left), significant-change location monitoring (the app is woken once you have moved far enough), and region monitoring around the places on your wishlist and the ones you log most often (80 m circles, at most 20 at a time). The app does not run continuous location updates and does not record your movement.
  • When you arrive somewhere, the app takes one high-accuracy location fix (it waits at most 20 seconds) to work out which place you are at. The coordinates that Visit Monitoring reports are often off by more than a hundred metres.
  • It requires Always location authorization and notification permission. If either is refused, the switch returns to off.
  • Three things can tell the app you have left: a departure from Visit Monitoring, a location change at least 150 m from where you stopped (further when the location accuracy is poor), or leaving one of those place circles. It then decides on your device whether to ask you anything at all: whether the stay lasted between 15 minutes and 4 hours, whether there were food places nearby, whether you already logged a meal during that time, how many times it has asked today, and whether it is the middle of the night.
  • Looking up nearby places sends that visit's coordinates to Apple's map service, for the same purpose as the nearby list when you log a meal (see “Maps”). The results do not leave your device.
  • The notification asks whether you want to log the meal. Tapping it only pre-fills the logging form; it does not create a record.
  • What the app keeps on the device: the switch's state, how many times it has asked today, identifiers for the last 40 visits (coordinates rounded to four decimals, time rounded down to the minute) so that the same visit is not raised twice, up to 12 open stays for at most 24 hours (arrival time, coordinates and the nearby places found), one pair of coordinates from the most recent location update (used to decide which places to draw circles around), and the last 300 events (arrivals, departures, location updates, region crossings, notifications), each carrying the coordinates and location accuracy at the time, used to check the feature's own reasoning. None of this leaves your device or is written into your records.
  • It can be turned off in the app under Settings › Reminders. Turning it off stops all location monitoring.

Photos

  • The photo library is opened through the system picker, so the app only receives the images you select. It does not browse your library and has never requested full photo library access.
  • Selected images are resized and copied into the app's own local storage, and displayed from there.
  • When you back-date an older meal from a picked photo, the app reads that photo's own capture date and GPS coordinates: the date pre-fills the meal's date, and the coordinates are used to look up nearby places. This happens entirely on your device. Neither the photo nor that information is uploaded, and the photo's GPS coordinates are not stored in the database.
  • The app does not write photos back to your photo library.

Maps

Map display, place search, administrative-district lookup and the navigation hand-off use Apple's built-in map services (MapKit and CoreLocation). Three things cause the system to send a request to Apple:

  1. Displaying the map (fetching map tiles).
  2. Searching for a place — what you type, along with the current map region, is sent to Apple to return suggestions and a list of nearby restaurants.
  3. A place you created yourself that lacks city/district information — its coordinates are sent to Apple to get the district name back.

These requests are handled by Apple and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer neither receives nor sees them. Apart from that, the app does not connect to any server for any reason — the developer operates none.

Backup and export

  • Export happens only when you tap it.
  • The resulting file is handed to the system share sheet. Where it goes and who gets it is entirely your decision.
  • Backup files are not password-protected — they contain your records and photos, so store them where you are comfortable.
  • The developer never receives the file. There is no automatic upload and no cloud sync (the app does not use iCloud sync).

What this app does not have

No accounts, no login, no advertising, no in-app analytics, no crash-reporting service, no push notifications, no App Tracking Transparency tracking, no advertising identifier or any user identifier, no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs, and no selling or sharing of data with anyone.

The only third-party code in the app is the open-source compression library ZIPFoundation (MIT licence), used solely to write and read backup files. It makes no network connections.

Children

This app is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, children included.

Map data sources and licence

The village-boundary and water-body data bundled with the app comes from Taiwan's Ministry of the Interior, National Land Surveying and Mapping Center: the Village (Li) Boundary Map, TWD97 (data dated 2026-08-17) and the Topographic Map Digital Data, 1:25,000. Both are provided under the Open Government Data License, Taiwan, version 1.0, and are used with attribution under that licence; the boundary geometry has been simplified for use in the app. The data is bundled in the app; no network access is needed to use it.
