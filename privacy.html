食霧

記錄自己吃過的每一家店和每道菜。

隱私權政策

「食霧」不收集你的任何資料。

這個 App 沒有帳號、沒有註冊、沒有伺服器、沒有廣告、沒有分析工具、沒有追蹤，也沒有任何第三方追蹤或分析 SDK。你記下的每一家店、每一道菜、每一張照片，都存在你自己的手機裡，開發者無從得知。

資料存在哪裡

所有記錄——店家與評分、每一餐、點了什麼、菜的評價與價錢份數、心得、同行者、菜單、想去的店——都存在你這支手機的 App 本機資料庫。照片（食物、菜單）存在 App 自己的檔案空間（Application Support），資料庫裡只記檔名。偏好設定（常一起吃的人、排序方式、已達成的里程碑）存在本機的 UserDefaults。

這些資料不會離開你的裝置，除非你自己按下匯出。刪除 App 就會一併刪掉這些資料，開發者手上不會留下任何副本。

定位

第一次打開 App 時，會詢問使用 App 期間（When In Use）的定位權限。位置用在兩件事：地圖上用藍點顯示你在哪裡，以及記一餐時列出附近的店家。

  • 地圖畫面開著時，系統會持續更新藍點的位置。
  • 列附近的店家時，App 取一次目前位置，精度約 100 公尺。
  • App 沒有背景執行模式。離開 App 之後，App 不會取得位置。
  • 你選定店家之後，存進記錄的是那家店的座標，不是你的座標。
  • 自己建立一家地圖上找不到的店時，可以把它標在現在的位置、照片拍攝的位置，或地圖上自己點的位置。標在現在的位置時，存的就是你當下的座標（約 100 公尺精度）。畫面上會寫出這家店標在哪裡。這筆座標只存在你的裝置上。
  • 在系統的「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 定位服務」可以關掉。關掉之後 App 一樣能用，店家要自己搜尋，地圖上也不會有藍點。

照片

照片來自你從相簿挑的照片，或用相機當下拍的照片，包括食物和菜單。

  • 相簿是透過系統的照片選取器開啟的，App 只拿得到你選的那幾張，不會瀏覽你的相簿，也從來沒有要求過相簿的完整讀取權限。
  • 選到的照片會縮圖後複製一份到 App 的本機檔案空間，之後在 App 內顯示的是這一份。
  • 從相簿補記舊帳時，App 會讀取照片本身帶的拍攝日期與 GPS 座標：日期用來自動帶入那一餐的日期，座標用來找出照片附近有哪些店。這個讀取全部在你的裝置上完成，照片與其中的資訊不會被上傳。照片的 GPS 座標不會存進資料庫；只有你把自訂店家標在照片的位置時，那個座標會成為這家店的座標。
  • App 不會把你的照片寫回相簿。分享卡可以從分享面板存進相簿，第一次存時系統會詢問「加入照片」的權限。

地圖

地圖畫面、附近的店家、搜尋店家、地圖上點店、反查行政區與導航入口，使用的是 Apple 內建的地圖服務（MapKit 與 CoreLocation）。店名、地址、類別這些店家資料來自 Apple 地圖。以下幾種情況會由系統向 Apple 發出請求：

  1. 顯示地圖（取得地圖圖磚）。
  2. 列附近的店家時，你目前的位置會送給 Apple，換回附近的餐廳清單。
  3. 搜尋店家時，你打的字與當時的位置範圍會送給 Apple，換回建議與店家清單。
  4. 在地圖上點一家店時，系統會向 Apple 查這家店的地址與行政區。
  5. 你自己建立的店家若缺縣市／行政區資料，該店座標會送給 Apple 換回行政區名稱。

這些請求由 Apple 處理，受 Apple 的隱私權政策規範。開發者不會收到、也看不到這些請求的內容。

除此之外，這個 App 不會為了任何理由連上任何伺服器——開發者沒有伺服器。

備份與匯出

App 可以把全部記錄匯出成一個 JSON 檔，或把記錄與照片打包成一個完整備份檔，也可以把備份檔匯入還原。

  • 匯出只在你主動按下的時候發生。
  • 產生的檔案會交給系統的分享面板，存到哪裡、傳給誰，完全由你決定。
  • 備份檔沒有密碼保護，內容就是你的記錄與照片，請自行決定要放在哪裡。
  • 開發者不會收到這個檔案，也沒有任何自動上傳或雲端同步（App 沒有使用 iCloud 同步）。

這個 App 沒有的東西

沒有帳號系統、沒有登入、沒有廣告、沒有應用程式內分析、沒有崩潰回報服務、沒有推播通知、沒有 App 追蹤透明度（ATT）追蹤、沒有廣告識別碼或任何使用者識別碼、沒有第三方追蹤或分析 SDK，也不會把資料賣給或分享給任何人。

App 內使用的唯一第三方程式碼是開源壓縮函式庫 ZIPFoundation（MIT 授權），只用來產生與讀取備份檔，不會連上網路。

兒童

這個 App 不針對兒童設計，也不會刻意收集任何人的資料（包含兒童）。

圖資來源與授權

App 內建的村里界線與水域圖資來自：

  • 內政部國土測繪中心「村(里)界圖(TWD97經緯度)」（圖資日期 2026-08-17）
  • 內政部國土測繪中心「經建版地形圖數值資料檔(比例尺二萬五千分之一)」

兩者均依「政府資料開放授權條款第 1 版」提供，本 App 依該條款標示出處使用，並對原始圖資做過簡化處理。這些圖資已內建在 App 裡，使用時不需要連線取得。

Privacy Policy (English)

Shiwu does not collect any of your data.

There is no account, no sign-up, no server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. Every place, dish, note and photo you record stays on your own device. The developer has no way to see any of it.

Where your data lives

All of your records — places and their ratings, meals, what you ordered, dish ratings with prices and portions, notes, companions, menus, wishlist entries — are stored in the app's local database on your device. Photos (food and menus) are stored in the app's own file storage (Application Support); the database only keeps file names. Preferences (frequent dining companions, sort order, milestones reached) are stored locally in UserDefaults.

None of this leaves your device unless you choose to export it. Deleting the app deletes this data. The developer keeps no copy.

Location

On first launch the app asks for When In Use location access. Your location is used for two things: showing where you are on the map (the blue dot), and listing nearby restaurants when you log a meal.

  • While the map is on screen, the system keeps the blue dot up to date.
  • To list nearby restaurants, the app takes one location fix at roughly 100 metres accuracy.
  • The app has no background modes. It does not get your location after you leave the app.
  • Once you pick a place, what gets saved with the record is that place's coordinates, not yours.
  • When you create a place the map doesn't know, you can mark it at your current location, at the location where a photo was taken, or at a spot you pick on the map. If you mark it at your current location, your coordinates at that moment (~100 m accuracy) become the place's coordinates. The screen shows where the place is marked. Those coordinates stay on your device.
  • Location can be turned off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services. The app still works; you search for places by name, and the map shows no blue dot.

Photos

Photos come from images you pick from your library, or photos you take with the camera, including food and menus.

  • The photo library is opened through the system picker, so the app only receives the images you select. It does not browse your library and has never requested full photo library access.
  • Selected images are resized and copied into the app's own local storage, and displayed from there.
  • When you back-date an older meal from a picked photo, the app reads that photo's own capture date and GPS coordinates: the date pre-fills the meal's date, and the coordinates are used to look up nearby places. This happens entirely on your device. Neither the photo nor that information is uploaded. The photo's GPS coordinates are not stored in the database, unless you mark a place you created at the photo's location; then those coordinates become the place's coordinates.
  • The app does not write your photos back to your library. You can save a share card to Photos from the share sheet; the first time, the system asks for permission to add photos.

Maps

Map display, nearby restaurants, place search, tapping a place on the map, administrative-district lookup and the navigation hand-off use Apple's built-in map services (MapKit and CoreLocation). Place details such as name, address and category come from Apple Maps. These cause the system to send a request to Apple:

  1. Displaying the map (fetching map tiles).
  2. Listing nearby restaurants — your current location is sent to Apple to get a list of nearby places.
  3. Searching for a place — what you type, along with the current map region, is sent to Apple to return suggestions and matching places.
  4. Tapping a place on the map — the system asks Apple for that place's address and district.
  5. A place you created yourself that lacks city/district information — its coordinates are sent to Apple to get the district name back.

These requests are handled by Apple and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer neither receives nor sees them. Apart from that, the app does not connect to any server for any reason — the developer operates none.

Backup and export

  • Export happens only when you tap it.
  • The resulting file is handed to the system share sheet. Where it goes and who gets it is entirely your decision.
  • Backup files are not password-protected — they contain your records and photos, so store them where you are comfortable.
  • The developer never receives the file. There is no automatic upload and no cloud sync (the app does not use iCloud sync).

What this app does not have

No accounts, no login, no advertising, no in-app analytics, no crash-reporting service, no push notifications, no App Tracking Transparency tracking, no advertising identifier or any user identifier, no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs, and no selling or sharing of data with anyone.

The only third-party code in the app is the open-source compression library ZIPFoundation (MIT licence), used solely to write and read backup files. It makes no network connections.

Children

This app is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, children included.

Map data sources and licence

The village-boundary and water-body data bundled with the app comes from Taiwan's Ministry of the Interior, National Land Surveying and Mapping Center: the Village (Li) Boundary Map, TWD97 (data dated 2026-08-17) and the Topographic Map Digital Data, 1:25,000. Both are provided under the Open Government Data License, Taiwan, version 1.0, and are used with attribution under that licence; the boundary geometry has been simplified for use in the app. The data is bundled in the app; no network access is needed to use it.
